# Experiment Backlog & Evidence (Q3 2026)

Candidate improvements, ranked by projected weekly-active-user impact. Figures are
lifts on top of the Q3 WAU baseline (84,200) from controlled experiments and
benchmarks.

## 1. Cut onboarding to 5 steps with in-app showing of the "aha" value (highest confidence)
- Expected WAU retention lift: +4 to +6 points on week-4 retention.
- Strongest evidence: W40 onboarding experiment already raised W1 retention +6pts.
- Exit survey: 34% say setup takes too long; 26% don't understand value.

## 2. Add a re-engagement touch at day 5-7 (push/SMS if opted in, else email digest)
- Expected: recover ~9% of week-3 lapsed users (benchmark cohort studies 2025-2026).
- Current gap: no touch between day 0 activation email and "week 3" nudge.

## 3. Turn "referral activated" users into a referral loop (friend-refer-friend with kicker)
- Referral-activated users retain 20pts higher at week 4 than organic (58% vs 38%).
- Projected: +2 to +3 WAU growth from compounding referral effects.

## What NOT to ship yet
- Heavy gamification (badges/streaks): Q3 tests showed no significant retention lift.
