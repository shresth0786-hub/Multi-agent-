# Product Feature Notes (current build notes)

## Mobile app
- iOS + Android, React Native 0.76. Offline-first dashboard.
- Known mobile performance issue under heavy widget load (reported by 12% of churn survey).

## Onboarding
- Current flow is 9 screens. W40 experiment reduced it to 5 and improved W1 retention.
- "Aha" screen shows a 10-second example of a completed dashboard.

## Notifications & re-engagement
- 1 activation email on day 0 (transactional, essential).
- Email digest option exists for weekly summary; opt-in rate 22%.
- No push/SMS automation wired to churn risk signals yet.

## Referrals
- "Invite a teammate" exists (referral-activated users: 58% W4 retention).
- No rewarded kicker program live yet.
