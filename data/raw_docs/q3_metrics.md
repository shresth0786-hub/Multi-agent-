# Q3 2026 Product Metrics

## Headline numbers (Q3 2026)
- Weekly Active Users (WAU): 84,200 (Q3 close) vs 61,000 (Q2 close): +38% quarter over quarter.
- Monthly churn rate: 6.8% (down from 8.9% in Q2).
- DAU/MAU ratio: 31% (Q2: 27%).
- Median session length: 4.7 minutes (Q2: 3.9 minutes).
- North Star metric, WAU, grew in all 13 weeks of the quarter; largest jump in week 40 (onboarding experiment shipped).

## Cohort retention (weekly, signup week -> retained)
- Users who complete onboarding (7 steps or fewer) retain at 62% in week 4.
- Users who skip onboarding retain at 29% in week 4.
- Week-1 retention overall: 71%. Week-4 retention overall: 41%.
- Referral-activated users retain at 58% in week 4, vs 38% for organic signups.
